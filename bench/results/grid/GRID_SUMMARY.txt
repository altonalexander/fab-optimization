GRID SUMMARY: where rules-based dispatch breaks down. FINAL for the rule grid, 2026-09-17 ~07:00Z.
SMT2020 LVHM, PySCFabSim, scrap on first queue-time violation, Demand batching, QT_PROMOTE_FRAC=0.50,
batch-tier fix on (qp050b), load 1.0. 60-day window after a 90-day warm-up, 5 seeds unless noted.
Rules: qt (queue-time sort key); qtfK6 (qt + feed-the-batch, lookahead 6); qtfwK6s8 (qtfK6 + fire an
underfilled batch when a member has < 8 h of window left); fifo; cr.
Metrics: good/d = shipped lots/day. share = scrapped / (shipped + scrapped). CT = shipped lots only
(survivor-biased). drift = (last-third mean WIP - first-third mean WIP) per day.
VIABLE = drift < +5/day on every seed AND scrap share <= 15 % on every seed.

!! DECLARED DEVIATION: qtfw fires batches BELOW the dataset's batch_min. That is realistic fab practice
   (minimum batch with a window exception) but not SMT2020 as published. It needs USER SIGN-OFF before
   it can be the main result. Plain qt is the dataset-faithful rule.

!! WARM-UP BIAS: every cell resumes a warm-up, which is qt-built unless the rule name ends in Wqtfw.
   qtfw keeps alive lots the qt warm-up would scrap, so WIP re-equilibrates UPWARD during the window
   (scale 3: ~1,500 -> ~1,830). On qt warm-ups qtfw's drift test fails at 4/3/2 and its 60-day scrap share
   is optimistic (scale 3: 9.6 % qt-warmed vs 12.2 % qtfw-warmed; scale 2: 21.8 % vs 23.5 %).
   HEADLINE qtfw rows at scales 4, 3 and 2 therefore use qtfw-warmed checkpoints (Wqtfw). The same bias
   produced the crit v3 "win" at scale 3: see docs/notes/2026-09-17-where-rules-break.md.

Read:
- Plain dispatch rules (qt, qtfK6) hold to scale 5 and break at 4, on scrap, not on flow.
- The under-min exception (qtfw) moves the break to between 4 and 3; at 3-2 it breaks (12-24 % scrap).
  That is the CRITICAL RANGE for an optimiser.
- fifo and cr are not viable at any scale under queue-time constraints (fifo fails even at 8, one seed 22 %).
- qtfK6 is level with qt: feed-the-batch alone buys nothing at 60 d.
- 90-d confirmations (n=2) agree with 60-d verdicts at 8, 5 and 4. qt-warmed shares run ~2 points higher at 90 d.
